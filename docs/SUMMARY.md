# Lucent: what version 1 is

**Lucent is a private budget app for your phone and computer.** Everything you enter stays on your own device, locked with encryption. There's no account, no cloud and no internet needed.

## What it does
- **Log money in and out.** Amount, category, date and an optional note. Adding an expense takes a few taps.
- **Your own categories.** Nothing is filled in for you. When you first open the app, you create your own spending and income categories.
- **Monthly budgets.** Set a limit per category and see what's left. Each month starts fresh on the 1st, and you can look back at any past month.
- **Savings goals.** Money you put toward a goal shows as "Saved", separate from spending.
- **Repeating bills and income.** Enter rent or salary once and Lucent adds it for you each time. It reminds you the day before a bill is due.
- **Simple reports.** Money in, out and saved each month, and where your spending went.

## The screens
1. **Setup:** welcome, choose your currency, create your categories, a note about backups.
2. **Home:** this month at a glance (in, out, saved), budgets, bills coming up.
3. **Transactions:** everything you've logged, by day, with search.
4. **Add entry:** a big number pad plus a category list.
5. **Budgets, Goals, Repeating items, Reports.**
6. **Send / Receive:** move your data to your other devices.
7. **Backups and Settings:** including light or dark look.

## Moving data between your devices
- **Over home Wi-Fi.** Open "Receive" on one device and "Send" on the other. The first time, you scan a QR code or check that two 6-digit codes match. After that, the devices trust each other.
- **By file.** If the Wi-Fi blocks it, Lucent saves a locked file you can move any way you like (AirDrop, USB, a message) and open with your password.
- **You always see a preview** before anything is added. Sending the same thing twice never creates duplicates.
- **Tip:** set up your second device by receiving from the first, so both share the same categories.

## Keeping your data safe
- Your data is locked to the device. If the phone is lost or reset, **only a backup can bring it back.**
- Lucent reminds you to back up monthly. You can also have it save locked backups to a folder you choose, automatically.
- There's no fingerprint or face lock. Anyone who can unlock your device can open the app.

## How you install it (no app stores yet)
- **Android:** install the app file directly.
- **iPhone:** installed with a free tool (AltStore or SideStore) and your Apple ID. It must be refreshed **every 7 days** or it stops opening (your data stays). This is Apple's rule.
- **Mac and Windows:** the system warns that it's from an unknown developer, and you choose to open it anyway (on a Mac, in System Settings). Expect this warning again after updates.

## How it looks
Calm and simple. Light and dark modes, plain backgrounds, one blue accent, no gradients or heavy shadows. Numbers come first: big and clear, in the Inter typeface, with digits that line up neatly. Colors stay gentle, with no alarming reds and greens. Text grows with your phone's text-size setting.

## Not in version 1
Sharing a budget with family, rolling unused money into next month, other languages, receipt photos, tags or payees, and app-store downloads. These may come later.
